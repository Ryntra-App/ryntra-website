# Ryntra website product context

## Register

Brand. This is the official product website for Ryntra, with functional release,
download and documentation surfaces.

## Product purpose

Ryntra is a native Android and iOS workspace for Modrinth creators. It brings
project management, releases, analytics, organizations, invitations and
notifications into one mobile product. The website explains that product,
proves it with real interface captures and gives visitors a dependable path to
download the current release.

## Target users

- Modrinth creators maintaining one or more projects.
- Team and organization owners coordinating releases and contributors.
- Existing Ryntra users checking releases, documentation or platform support.
- Prospective users deciding whether the app fits their publishing workflow.

## Primary outcomes

1. Understand what Ryntra does within the first viewport.
2. See the real Android and iOS product rather than fabricated UI.
3. Download the correct release with confidence.
4. Inspect changelogs and documentation without leaving the product site.

## Brand personality

- Native: familiar platform behavior and system-led hierarchy.
- Calm: low visual noise, direct language and restrained motion.
- Capable: real product depth is visible without exaggerated claims.
- Open: GitHub, releases and contributors are presented transparently.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with oversized display headlines.
- Editorial product-launch pages that make typography louder than the product.
- Decorative glass, floating badges, tilted devices and background ornaments.
- Fake dashboards, invented metrics, testimonials or partner logos.
- Literal replicas of an iPhone or Android screen used as the entire website.

## Strategic design principles

1. The product interface is the strongest brand asset.
2. Native-inspired means system typography, surfaces, controls and behavior,
   not imitating one operating system everywhere.
3. Desktop should feel like a composed Ryntra workspace; mobile should feel
   touch-first and compact, not like a squeezed desktop poster.
4. Use green as a functional accent, not as decorative atmosphere.
5. Prefer alignment, spacing and separators over cards and visual effects.
6. Every claim must be supported by an actual product capability or screenshot.

## Accessibility and platform requirements

- Follow system light or dark appearance by default with a persistent override.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus and semantic landmarks.
- Respect reduced motion.
- Keep touch targets at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- Support narrow phones, tablets, laptops and wide desktop viewports without
  horizontal overflow.
